The Inter IIIT Sports Meet will be organized annually among all IIITs in
rotation / mutual consent at any of the Indian Institute(s) of Information
Technology.

## I. Aims and Objectives

1. The main objective of Inter IIIT Sports Meet is to organize sports
   competitions in select National recognized events, to encourage
   sportsmanship, to promote and create better harmony, understanding and
   friendly interaction amongst the students of the IIITs as well as to
   establish a close association among them.
2. To create awareness on physical fitness and sports and games in day-to-day
   life.
3. To equip students with skills not only in sports, games and exercises but
   also to handle real life situations.
4. To raise the standard of sports in IIITs and to work for the development of
   character and values through sports among the students of IIITs, and hence
   contribute to sports in the country.
5. To discuss matters related to the organization of the sports and other such
   activities as may be supportive to the aims and objectives of the Inter IIIT
   Sports Meet, as mentioned above.
6. The Sports Meet shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions, which
   are formulated from time to time in agreement of all IIITs.

## II. Inter IIIT Sports Board

The Sports Board shall be constituted for the purpose of effective supervision
of the Inter IIIT Sports Meet. The headquarters of the Sports Board will be
located at the particular IIIT where the Inter IIIT Sports Meet is to be
organized that year. The Sports Board is constituted with the membership as
follows:

1. The Director of the host IIIT is the Chairman of the Sports Board.
2. Dean Students Affairs and one faculty in-charge of sports from each IIIT.
3. One senior staff member from the Physical Education Section of each IIIT.
4. One student General Secretary / Sports Secretary from each IIIT.

The Dean Student Affairs shall be the convener of the sports meet and the
faculty in-charge for sports of the host IIIT shall be the Co-Convener of the
sports meet. Member of the Physical Education Staff(s) of the host IIIT shall
be the Organizing Secretary of the Sports Meet. The Chairman of the Sports
Board shall preside over all Sports Board's meetings. In his/her absence, the
convener of Sports Board shall occupy the chair and conduct the proceedings of
the meetings. The minutes of all the meetings shall be recorded and circulated
to the members of all IIITs immediately after each meeting.

The quorum (the minimum number of members of an assembly or society that must
be present in person or by wire at any of its meetings to make the proceedings
of that meeting valid) for conduct of the Sports Board meeting shall be 13/25
of the total members of the Sports Board. All proceedings shall be decided by a
majority of votes. In case of a tie, the Chairman or his nominees shall make
their casting vote. The Sports Board of the Inter IIIT Sports Meet may co-opt
any individual in an advisory capacity, if needed, to be a member on an ad-hoc
basis, without any voting right.

The Sports Board shall meet at the host IIIT or via virtual mode, as soon as
possible, after the academic session begins for the year of conduct of the
Inter IIIT Sports Meet. The Board shall have 2 more mandatory meetings, one on
the day preceding the inauguration of the Sports Meet and the other before the
closing ceremony. Additional meetings shall be convened as desired by the
Board.

In pursuance of the responsibilities vested in the Board, it will have the
following specific powers in conduct of the Sports Meet:

1. To co-ordinate the Sports Meet, finalize the dates, draws & fixtures of the
   Meet.
2. Addition or deletion of any event.
3. To amend, modify, formulate the Inter IIIT Sports Meet rules with majority
   of votes among the members present.
4. Apart from above mentioned powers, the board will be the final authority to
   decide any matters arising during the Sports Meet and its decisions shall be
   binding on all the members.
5. To discuss the matters related to the organization of sports and to promote
   other such activities that may be conducive to the aims and objectives of
   the Inter IIIT Sports Meet.
6. All the competitions will be conducted as per the federation rules for each
   game and sports implemented by Indian Federations till date.

## V. Schedule and Draws

The host IIIT shall run all the events in accordance with the schedule of date
and time as approved by the Sports Board. The participating IIIT contingent
list will be sent to the Organizing Secretary well in advance to ensure smooth
and organized conduct of the Meet as per the decided schedule. The Winners and
Runners-Up may be referred to as W, R1, R2 and R3 in order of the position
acquired by the teams in their particular sport held during the previous Sports
Meet. The pools for the remaining teams would be decided by drawing lots for
individual games during the first Board Meeting. If one team withdraws its
entry from the pool of 4 teams, no redraw of fixture will be done. In all other
cases of withdrawal of two or more teams redrawing will be done and the
previous fixture will be considered as null and void. However, the organizing
committee may not choose to go for the pooling as mentioned above and choose a
different method for fixtures, if there are limited registrations or time
constraints. The Organizing committee reserves the right to alternate scheduled
program under unusual circumstances.

## VII. Trophies and Certificates

Overall Championship trophies of the Inter IIIT Sports Meet will be rolling
trophies and a stock list of trophies available for award shall be maintained
by the Sports Board and printed as an annexure from time to time in the
handbook of competition rules for the Inter IIIT Sports Meet (see Annexure I).

In team games, trophies, certificates of merit and medals shall be awarded to
the winners, the 1st runners up and the 2nd runners up only. The certificates
signed by the Chairman, the Convener, Co-Convener and the Organizing Secretary
of the Inter IIIT Sports Board shall be awarded. In individual events like
Athletics, Powerlifting etc. medals shall be awarded to the top 3 positions and
the merit certificates shall be awarded up to the 4th position.

In the case of athletics (M & W), best athlete certificates and trophy will be
awarded while for powerlifting, best powerlifter certificate and trophy shall
be awarded. No IIIT shall award certificates of its own to any of its
participants in the Inter IIIT Sports Meet. Only the Inter IIIT Sports Board is
authorized to award certificates of Merit. The logo of Inter IIIT Sports Meet
must be used in medals and certificates. Best players of various sports and
games shall be decided by a panel of experts (PTIs) from select IIITs attending
the meet including the host as the Chairman.

## VIII. Officials for the Meet

Organizing IIIT will be responsible for selecting competent umpires/referees
whose decisions on all points of fact shall be final and binding. The panel of
umpires/referees shall be drawn up by the host IIIT well in advance. Only
qualified Association / Federation referees and umpires shall be appointed to
supervise the Inter IIIT Sports Meet. In case of non-availability of
Association Umpires, the Organizing Secretary shall have the power to appoint
suitable officials having no official association with the Host IIIT.

## IX. Conduct / Protest

For all games and sports, the decision of the referees and the umpires will be
final and binding. Therefore, any team leaving the field of play as a mark of
protest and conceding a walk-over shall be deemed to have lost the fixture and
will be eliminated from rest of the event. Only the remaining teams will be
considered as participating in that event. Protest against the decision of an
umpire/referee on a point of rule and any other protest in connection with the
concerned match / events shall be given in writing addressed to the Jury of
Appeal (set by the sports board) along with a protest fee of Rs 1000/-.

The written protest must reach the Organizing Secretary of the host institute
within an hour of the conclusion of the concerned game duly signed by the
competent authority of that particular IIIT. The matter will be referred to a
committee which has representation from select IIITs.

A team/competitor shall be permitted a grace of 10 minutes from the scheduled
timing of the event and then a walk-over shall be awarded unless genuine
reasons are presented and accepted by the board.

## X. Disruption / Postponement

Any event abandoned or disrupted due to unavoidable reasons shall be postponed
or continued, as the case may be, and as per rules specified in the particular
events. In case the Federation/Association rules cannot be implemented, the
decision will be left to the Board whose ruling shall be final.

## XI. Expenses

Each IIIT should pay the registration fee of Rs 2000 + 18% GST per participant
(subject to change on an annual basis, with the approval of the Sports Board).
Each IIIT shall bear the travelling and incidental expenses of its own teams.
The host IIIT shall provide the boarding and lodging facilities for visiting
teams.

**Medical:** The host IIIT will provide first aid and will take care of the
injured contingent for further treatment during any emergency, but the actual
bill of treatment at an outside hospital is to be borne by the respective IIIT.
In this connection a refundable caution deposit of Rs 25,000/- must be paid. If
medical expenses go beyond Rs 25,000/- then the concerned IIIT will settle the
amount against a limited loan given by the host IIIT. The registration fee and
caution deposit should be paid online, preferably by bank transfer or UPI, to
the account details provided at the fest website (twaran25.iiitm.ac.in in the
source PDF).

## XII. General Rules

a) Total contingent size per IIIT: as shown in Table 1.

b) The Board, in its first meeting, will decide about the equipment/brands to be
used for the Inter IIIT Sports Meet. The equipment approved in the previous
meet will be followed to the extent possible, with changes approved by the

c) The order of events / fixtures will be fixed and notified well in advance to

d) Host IIIT will be providing the latest federation rule book of all the games

photo, which should be endorsed by the Dean of Students of the respective

g) Maximum of 2 officials (Faculty In-Charge for Sports / Sports Officers /

h) It is compulsory for all contingent members to be present during the March
Past. Non-compliance with the same will lead to disciplinary action by the
Inter IIIT Sports Board and its decision will be binding for all contingent
members.

i) If needed, the rule book of Inter IIIT Sports Meet will be upgraded every
five years.

## XIV. Badminton

a) The competition shall be conducted under the International Badminton
Federation rules as adopted from time to time by the Badminton Association of
India, unless otherwise modified.

b) The tournament for men and women sections will be conducted on the lines of
the Inter State Badminton Championship. Ties for men and women will be
separately decided according to the pattern given below.

c) The tie for men shall be decided by the combined result of 3 singles and 2
doubles and for women by the results of 2 singles and one doubles.

d) Result (Men and Women) of best of three sets (each set of 21 points).

e) The order of events shall be as follows:
- **Men:** First Singles / Second Singles / First Doubles / Third Singles /
  Second Doubles
- **Women:** First Singles / Doubles / Second Singles

f) Number of players:
- **Men:** a maximum of 5 members in a team.
- **Women:** a maximum of 3 members in a team.

g) A tie (final scores level) will be resolved by successively applying the
following criteria:
1. Individual matches won / individual matches lost.
2. Games won by the team / games lost by the team.
3. Points for / points against.

The tally for all the matches played in the league fixture will be considered.

## XV. Basketball

a) The tournament will be conducted according to the International Basketball
Federation Rules as adopted from time to time by the Indian Basketball
Federation unless otherwise modified.

b) The number of players representing any IIIT will not be more than 12 in case
of men and not more than 12 certificates will be awarded to a team.

c) The number of players representing any IIIT will not be more than 10 in case
of women and not more than 10 certificates will be awarded to a team.

d) During matches, if the score is the same after the end of the scheduled time,
Basketball Federation Rules will be applied, i.e. the game shall continue with
as many extra periods of 5 minutes as are necessary to break the tie.

e) On completion of the league matches, if two or more teams are on the same
points, the tie shall be resolved as follows:
1. If two teams are tied, head-to-head wins among the teams will be considered
   to break the tie.
2. If more than two teams are equal in placing, a second classification will be
   established, taking into account only the results of the games between the
   teams that have tied.
3. If there are still teams tied after the second classification, the basket
   average will be used to determine the placing, taking into account only the
   results of the games between the teams that tied.
4. If there are still teams tied, the placing will be determined using the score
   average from the results of all their games played in the group.

## XVI. Carrom

a) The rules of the International Carrom Federation as adopted from time to time
by the All-India Carrom Federation shall be followed unless otherwise modified.

b) Each match shall be played following a best of three boards in league stages
and best of five boards for semifinals and final.

c) No. of players from each IIIT will be no more than 3.

d) The two singles players on one side are named A & B and the two singles
players on the other side are named X & Y.

e) The order of play for both men and women shall be 1st Singles / Doubles /
2nd Singles.

f) The Carrom tournament will be played on a league + knock-out basis.

g) Max timing: 60 minutes will be allocated for 1 match. In case the game does
not come to an end in the given time, the team/player with maximum points at
the end of 60 minutes will be adjudged winner. However, the last board being
played at that time will be completed.

h) All equipment (carrom board, coins, powder and striker) will be provided by
the host IIIT. Participants are allowed to use their own striker, but the
striker should match the specification as per the rules.

## XVII. Chess

a) The rules of the World Chess Federation as adopted from time to time by the
All-India Chess Federation shall be followed unless otherwise modified.

b) The Chess tournament will be played on the Swiss method.

c) No. of players from each IIIT will be no more than 4.

d) All matches will be conducted for a time duration of 1 hour. If the match
extends beyond the regular duration, 10 minutes of extra time will be given to
decide the winner. If the match still has not ended after the extra time, the
result will be decided by a coin toss between the players.

e) The ranking of the players shall be submitted to the organizers before the
start of the first round.

f) A chess clock shall be provided if needed.

## XVIII. Cricket

a) Matches shall be played according to the ICC rules in force, as adopted from
time to time by the Board of Control for Cricket in India, unless otherwise
modified.

b) All the matches will be conducted on a league cum knock-out basis and each
side is allowed to complete 12 or 15 overs in the league stage depending upon
time constraints, unless the opposing team is dismissed earlier. Maximum 2
bowlers can bowl 3 overs each in a 12 overs game. Each bowler can bowl a maximum
of 3 overs in 15 overs matches. The semi-finals and finals will be conducted for
either 15 overs or 20 overs, depending upon time constraints. Each bowler can
bowl a maximum of 4 overs in a 20 overs match. Matches will be conducted on a
matting pitch in day time only.

c) The bowling team is given 1 hour 10 minutes for 12 overs, 1 hour 30 minutes
for 15 overs and 2 hours for 20 overs to complete the innings. The penalty for
every short over will be decided by all captains prior to the meet.

d) If there is a tie, the team having the better net run rate shall be
considered for deciding placing.

e) The umpires are empowered to rearrange the number of overs by each side in
the event of a delayed start or if play is suspended. The number of overs for
the team batting second will not be reduced if the team batting first has been
dismissed in fewer than the agreed number of overs.

f) Each IIIT shall submit a list of players not exceeding 15 who may
participate in the tournament. No more than 15 certificates shall be awarded to
a team.

g) Rules regarding the postponement of a cricket match due to rain:
1. When rain affects play during the innings of the first team itself, the
   match will be continued from where it has been discontinued. The umpires
   will decide whether the ground is fit for play or not.
2. When rain affects play during the innings of the second team batting and
   the team batting second has not yet batted for half the number of overs, the
   match will be abandoned (points will be shared between the two teams).
3. If the team batting second has batted for more than half the overs and
   further play is not possible on the same day, the winner is decided to be the
   team with the better run rate at the point where the second team stopped
   batting.

h) A team will be awarded 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw and 0 for a lost
match.

## XIX. Kabaddi

a) The rules of the Kabaddi Federation of India as adopted from time to time
shall be followed unless otherwise modified.

b) A team shall consist of 12 players, of which 7 will be part of a team for a
given match. Substitution rules apply.

c) The weight of the player shall not be greater than 85 kg.

d) Duration of the match is 40 minutes, divided into two halves of 20 minutes
each with a 5-minute break.

e) A raider losing his cant in the opponent's court shall be out.

f) Do or Die raid and Super Tackle rules will be observed.

g) Substitution of players is not allowed during the tie-break and golden raid
plays. Substitutions of up to 5 players are allowed during half time.

h) In case of a tie, both the teams are given five raids by different raiders to
raid alternately.

i) One point will be given to the raider for crossing the bonus line while 6
players are on the ground. When 5 players or fewer are on the ground, bonus
points are not given.

j) A team will be awarded 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw and 0 for a lost
match.

## XX. Football

a) Matches shall be played according to the rules of FIFA as adopted from time
to time by the All-India Football Federation unless otherwise modified.

b) The duration of each match shall be 60 minutes (30-5-30). During the league
stage, after the expiry of playtime, if the tie still persists, the points
shall be shared equally.

c) Every IIIT should submit a list of players, not exceeding 16, who may
participate in the tournament. No more than 16 certificates shall be awarded to
a team. The matches could be conducted in either daytime or nighttime.

d) No extra time will be given in the knockout stage of football matches even if
the match is drawn. There will be a direct penalty shootout to decide the
winner.

e) The yellow cards given during the league stages will not be counted in the
knock-out stages.

f) A team will be awarded 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, and zero for a lost
match.

g) In case of a tie, goal difference (goals for minus goals against) of all the
matches played by the team shall be considered. If the tie still persists, the
number of goals for throughout the tournament shall be considered. If the tie
still persists, the number of goals against throughout the tournament shall be
considered.

## XXI. Powerlifting

a) The rules as adopted by the Indian Powerlifting Federation shall be followed,
unless otherwise modified in these rules.

b) The competition shall be held in the following weight categories:
- **Men:** up to 66 kg; 66.01 to 74 kg; 74.01 to 83 kg; 83.01 kg+
- **Women:** up to 52 kg; 52.01 to 63 kg; 63.01 to 72 kg; 72.01 kg+

c) No. of participants from each IIIT shall be a maximum of 2 per category.

d) Three chances shall be given to clear each weight.

e) There shall be three types of lifting — Squat, Bench Press and Deadlift. A
participant must lift in all these categories.

## XXII. Table Tennis

a) The rules of the International Table Tennis Federation as adopted from time
to time by the Table Tennis Federation of India shall apply unless otherwise
modified. The tournament shall be played in the Swaythling Cup method as
followed by the All India Inter University Tournament.

b) Men & Women matches will be held on the basis of five singles.

c) The order of play shall be as follows:
1. Men and Women: 1st Singles / 2nd Singles / 3rd Singles / 1st Reverse
   Singles / 2nd Reverse Singles.
2. A, B, C and X, Y, Z shall be determined by a draw of lots. The result of the
   match shall be declared when either of the teams wins 3 matches.
3. Each match will be played following a best of five games format, with each
   game of 11 points.
4. The number of players representing an IIIT shall not be more than three.
5. No more than three certificates will be awarded to a team.
6. A tie will be resolved by successively applying the following criteria:
   1. Individual matches won / individual matches lost.
   2. Games won by the team / games lost by the team.
   3. Points for / points against.
7. The score standings, when the match is decided, shall be considered for the
   calculations indicated above. The tally for all matches played among the
   tied teams in the league fixture will be considered.

## XXIII. Lawn Tennis

a) The rules of the International Tennis Federation as adopted from time to time
by the All-India Lawn Tennis Association shall apply, unless otherwise
modified. The tournament shall be played on the Davis Cup method as followed by
the All India Inter University Tournament. The order of play shall be — Men and
Women: First Singles / Doubles / Reverse Singles.

b) No. of players shall be not more than 3 in men and women.

c) Players playing at A, B and X, Y will be submitted by the captain before the
start of the match.

d) Each match will be the best of 11 games in league stages, and then best of
three sets will be played for semifinals and final. The tennis tie-break rules
shall be applied in all sets (7/12 points).

e) Ties will be resolved by successively applying the following criteria:
1. Individual matches won / individual matches lost.
2. Sets won by the team / sets lost by the team.
3. Points for / points against (set score to be taken).

f) The score standings, when the match is decided, shall be considered for the
calculations indicated above.

## XXIV. Volleyball

a) The rules of the International Volleyball Federation (FIVB) as adopted from
time to time by the Volleyball Federation of India (VFI) shall be followed
unless otherwise modified.

b) The number of players representing any IIIT will not be more than 12 in case
of men and not more than 12 certificates will be awarded to a team.

c) The number of players representing any IIIT will not be more than 10 in case
of women and not more than 10 certificates will be awarded to a team.

d) The tournament shall be played on a league cum knockout basis. All the
matches shall be played on the best of 5 sets for men, best of 3 sets for
women.

e) The following points system will be followed for the matches played on a
league basis:
1. Match won: 3 points
2. Match lost: 0 points
3. Technical forfeit: 1 point

f) Any team refusing to play a match without proper justification will be
eliminated from the competition and the results of the matches already played
will be cancelled.

g) The tally of all matches played in the league fixture will be considered.

h) The general rules for Volleyball (Women) are the same.

i) The game will be best of three sets.

## XXV. Squash

a) The rules of the World Squash Federation (WSF) shall be followed unless
otherwise modified.

b) Each set shall be played for 11 points.

c) The winner of each game is decided by the best of 3 sets for group matches
and best of 5 sets for semi-finals and finals.

d) The number of players in a team shall be a maximum of 3 for men and a
maximum of 3 for women.

e) A team will be awarded 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw and 0 for a lost
match.

f) The order of play will be as follows. 3 players on one side are labelled A,
B and C, and 3 players on the other are labelled X, Y and Z. The order of play
shall be A vs X, B vs Y, and C vs Z.

## XXVII. Tug of War

a) A maximum of 8 players are allowed in a team.

b) Either the pooling technique or round-robin technique will be used to
conduct the matches based on the number of registrations.

c) The winner of each match will be decided based on the best of 3 pulls.

d) The players should grab the rope with their bare hands. No knots or loops of
cloth are allowed on their hands. All the players should keep the rope on their
right-hand side; this is to ensure balance.

e) The rear-end puller of the team may serve as an "Anchor Man". He is allowed
to tie the rope to his waist and keep the rope diagonally over the left
shoulder.

## XXVIII. Kho-Kho

a) The game of Kho-Kho shall be played according to the rules approved by the
Kho-Kho Federation of India (KKFI), unless otherwise modified herein.

b) Each team shall consist of 12 players, of which 9 players will take the field
for a given match, while 3 will remain as substitutes.

c) A match shall consist of two innings for each team. Each inning shall be of 9
minutes chasing and 9 minutes defending, with an interval of 5 minutes between
the two innings. In case of a tie at the end of regular play, additional
innings will be played until a winner is determined as per KKFI rules.

d) Each defender successfully tagged by the chasers earns one point for the
chasing team. The team with the higher cumulative points at the end of the
match shall be declared the winner.

e) Substitutions are allowed during the match but only during intervals or in
case of injuries, subject to the referee's approval. A maximum of three
substitutions per match shall be permitted.

f) A team will be awarded 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, and zero for a lost
match.

g) Unsportsmanlike conduct or deliberate rule violation shall result in
disciplinary action, including disqualification or 1 point as a technical
point, as determined by the referee.

## XXIX. Disciplinary Rules

1. Being a member of the institutes of national importance, it is the
   responsibility of each and every player and spectator to uphold the sanctity
   of the IIITs. In this regard, a strict no hooting policy shall be issued by
   the host institute.
2. Any player or spectator (including those of the host IIIT) commenting
   maliciously / hooting maliciously at another IIIT shall be suspended
   immediately from the meet. Such a person will also be banned from playing or
   witnessing further events of the present meet. However, he can appeal to the
   disciplinary committee, and he won't be allowed unless the disciplinary
   committee decides in his favour.
3. In this regard, an undertaking will also be taken from each and every player
   of the IIITs.
4. However, the players and spectators are allowed to support their own team
   without commenting on the other IIIT.
5. A disciplinary committee will be constituted by the Sports Board consisting
   of at least one person from each IIIT and the decision of this committee will
   be binding on all IIITs.
6. The responsibilities of this committee will be to look after and maintain
   discipline during the Inter IIIT Sports Meet. In case of any unruly dissent
   or gesture against the conducting officials, the concerned IIIT shall be
   warned by the board. In case of further violation, the particular team of
   the concerned IIIT will be debarred from the Inter IIIT Meet.
7. Unruly behaviour of the student members of any IIIT contingent shall be
   generally dealt with by suitable punishment / debarring etc.
8. No members, including the Board members, will be allowed inside the playing
   arena/track during the meet.
9. Each IIIT will nominate one Sports Board member for the disciplinary
   committee.
10. Any point not directly covered by these rules shall be decided in the light
    of the rules most nearly applicable and in accordance with the general tenor
    and the spirit of the said rules.
